Our community

Rooted in faith.
Connected through community.

ICMC brings Muslims in McLean County together around prayer, learning, and service. Masjid Ibrahim is our shared place of worship in Bloomington.

Our work is guided by the Qur’an and the Sunnah. Through religious education and community services, we help make faith a meaningful part of everyday life.
