Our community

Serving McLean County since 2016

ICMC was established in 2016 through the merger of the Islamic Center of Bloomington-Normal and Masjid Ibrahim. The center brings local families and students together for worship, learning, and community activities.

Our work is guided by the Qur’an and the Sunnah. Through religious education and community services, we help make faith a meaningful part of everyday life.
