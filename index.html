Welcome to the
Islamic Center
Of McLean County

Islamic Center of McLean County (ICMC) is an Islamic community center located in Bloomington, IL.
Masjid Ibrahim, a part of ICMC, is the only full service masjid in a forty mile radius and serves the muslims of the county.
ICMC is governed by a Board of Directors and we are committed to preserving an Islamic identity, building and
supporting a viable Muslim community in our county and promoting a comprehensive Islamic way of life based on the Holy Quran and the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
