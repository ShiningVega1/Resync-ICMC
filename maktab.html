Maktab

After-school Qur’an learning and essential Islamic education.

Learning that supports daily life

ICMC’s Maktab program helps children develop their Qur’an recitation with tajwīd while learning the beliefs, practices, values, and manners central to Islam.

Areas of study

  • Qur’an recitation and pronunciation.
  • Foundational Islamic teachings.
  • Islamic values and everyday conduct.

The program is intended for school-going boys and girls in the community. Contact ICMC for current placement, schedule, and enrollment details.
