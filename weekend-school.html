Weekend Islamic School

An encouraging place for children to learn about their faith.

Islamic studies and Qur’an

The Weekend Islamic School introduces children to Islamic knowledge through a curriculum rooted in the Qur’an and Sunnah. Its aim is to create a welcoming and supportive setting for learning.

How learning is organized

The existing program information describes classes for children ages four and older, generally grouped by school grade. Qur’an placement is assessed separately so instruction can match each student’s level.

  • Islamic studies: grade-appropriate lessons using the Iqra Wise curriculum.
  • Qur’an: learning the Arabic alphabet, foundational reading with Noorani Qaida, Qur’an reading, and surah memorization.
Please confirm current class times, fees, and the school calendar with the school team. The calendar published on the source website is dated 2022.
