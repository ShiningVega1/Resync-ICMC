Sheikh Saad Palwala

Sheikh Saad Palwala is a Canadian-American Muslim scholar and preacher, born in Montreal. He has served communities in Ontario and Illinois as an imam and chaplain.

He studied at Jaamiah al-Uloom al-Islamiyyah in Toronto, completing his Islamic studies, then pursued advanced Hadith study at Darul Uloom Zakariya in South Africa.

His work includes teaching, public speaking, and community service. ICMC’s published biography identifies him as an imam and teacher at the center.
