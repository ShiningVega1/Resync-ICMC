About Imam Saad

Imam Saad

Imam Saad was born in Montreal, Quebec, and has served Muslim communities in Ontario and Illinois as an imam, teacher, and chaplain. His work centers on religious education, spiritual guidance, and supporting families and young people.

He began his Islamic studies at Jaamiah al-Uloom al-Islamiyyah in Toronto and later pursued advanced studies in Hadith at Darul Uloom Zakariya in South Africa.

Community Leadership

As imam and teacher at the Islamic Center of McLean County in Bloomington, Imam Saad leads prayers, delivers Friday sermons, teaches, and provides guidance to members of the community.

He has helped the community establish its home on Olympia Drive, with particular attention to creating opportunities for families and younger generations. He also takes part in local interfaith and civic gatherings, including events involving Not In Our Town Bloomington-Normal and Illinois State University.

Through his teaching, pastoral work, and community involvement, Imam Saad seeks to make the masjid a welcoming place for worship, learning, and connection.
