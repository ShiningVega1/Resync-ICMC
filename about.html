About Us

A community shaped by worship, learning, and the people who bring it together.

Our story

The Islamic Center of McLean County was formed in 2016 when the Islamic Center of Bloomington-Normal and Masjid Ibrahim joined together. That partnership brought the community’s worship, education, and gatherings into one organization.

Today, the center on Olympia Drive provides space for daily and Friday prayers, religious education, and community activities. ICMC also welcomes opportunities to share information about Islam and build understanding with neighbors of other faiths.

From ICMC’s existing website.

Decades of community life

1974 · Friday prayers begin
An Illinois State University instructor began organizing Jumu’ah prayers and sermons at the Campus Religious Center.
1980s–1990s · A growing network
Residents and students helped sustain prayers and gatherings. The Muslim Student Association at ISU was revived in 1995, followed by more activities bringing students and local families together.
2000 · A permanent place to gather
A committee of residents helped establish the Islamic Center of Bloomington-Normal, the area’s first mosque.
2016 · ICMC is formed
ICBN and Masjid Ibrahim merged to serve the growing community together.

A shared home for a diverse community

ICMC’s history includes families and individuals with roots across the United States, South Asia, Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia. Their different backgrounds are part of the center’s shared life.
